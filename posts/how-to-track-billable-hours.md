date: "2026-08-12"
summary: "Habits that make sure every billable minute ends up on an invoice."
---
Most freelancers lose billable time in small pieces: a five-minute email here, a quick call there.

## Start the timer before you start the work

Make it a habit: open the task, start the timer, then begin.

## Tag as you go

Tag client and project when you start, not at the end of the week when you've forgotten.

## Review every Friday

Ten minutes on Friday to fix gaps saves an afternoon at the end of the month.
