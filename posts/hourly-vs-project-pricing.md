---
title: "Hourly vs project pricing for freelancers"
date: "2026-09-03"
summary: "When each pricing model works, and how to switch."
---
Hourly pricing is fair when the scope is unclear. Project pricing rewards you for working efficiently.

## Charge hourly when

The client keeps changing the brief, or the work is support and maintenance.

## Charge per project when

You've done similar work many times and can estimate it well.

## Use your tracked hours

Your past time logs are the best guide to what a project price should be.
